Prayagraj Sangam Tour Plan

Prayagraj is known for Triveni Sangam, sacred snan, Hanuman Mandir, Akshay Vat, Anand Bhawan and important spiritual stops connected with Hindu pilgrimage. Our Prayagraj tour helps families and devotees travel comfortably from Ayodhya, Varanasi, Lucknow or nearby stations with private cab and hotel support.

You can book a one day Prayagraj local trip or combine Prayagraj with Ayodhya and Varanasi for a complete spiritual circuit. We help with pickup timing, route planning, temple stops and return travel so the day feels organized.

Tour Highlights

  • Triveni Sangam darshan and boat timing guidance
  • Bade Hanuman Mandir and Akshay Vat visit
  • Ayodhya Prayagraj cab service
  • Hotel and pickup support for families
  • Optional Varanasi and Chitrakoot extension
