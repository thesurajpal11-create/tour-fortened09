Naimisharanya Darshan Plan

Naimisharanya is a deeply sacred destination known for Chakra Tirth, Maa Lalita Devi Temple, Vyas Gaddi, Suta Gaddi, Hanuman Garhi and other devotional places. Our Naimisharanya tour helps visitors travel comfortably from Ayodhya, Lucknow or nearby cities with private cab and simple route planning.

The tour can be arranged as a same day trip or as part of an Ayodhya, Lucknow and Naimisharanya spiritual route. We help families choose practical timings, cab type and stay options if overnight travel is needed.

Tour Highlights

  • Chakra Tirth and Lalita Devi Temple darshan
  • Vyas Gaddi, Suta Gaddi and local sacred points
  • Cab service from Ayodhya or Lucknow
  • Hotel and pickup coordination
  • Family friendly spiritual itinerary
