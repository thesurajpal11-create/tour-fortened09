Ram Mandir Darshan Planning

Ram Mandir is the main reason many families visit Ayodhya, and the trip becomes easier when pickup, hotel location, local movement and timing are planned before arrival. Ayodhya Ramnagri Tourism helps guests plan Ram Mandir darshan with practical cab support, hotel booking guidance and local sightseeing around Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, Ram Ki Paidi and Saryu Ghat.

We keep the route simple for senior citizens, children and first-time visitors. Share your arrival station, airport or hotel location, group size and preferred travel date, and our team can suggest a comfortable darshan-focused plan.
