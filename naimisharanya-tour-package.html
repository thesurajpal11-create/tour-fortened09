Naimisharanya Tirth Plan

Naimisharanya is a quiet and powerful tirth destination for devotees who want a focused temple visit away from heavy city rush. Our Naimisharanya tour package can include Chakra Tirth, Lalita Devi Temple, Vyas Gaddi, Hanuman Garhi and nearby sacred points as per available time. We arrange cab pickup from Ayodhya, Lucknow, Sitapur or nearby routes and help with basic hotel guidance when an overnight halt is needed. The plan is useful for families, senior citizens and small groups who want clear timing, patient drivers and simple local coordination for puja and darshan. It can also be paired with Ayodhya for a compact pilgrimage.

Package Highlights

  • Chakra Tirth and Lalita Devi Temple visit
  • Cab pickup from nearby cities
  • Hotel assistance for overnight travel
  • Custom route with Ayodhya or Lucknow
